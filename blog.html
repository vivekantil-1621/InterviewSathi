Top 10 Valuation Interview Questions

Here are some of the most common valuation interview questions you should prepare for:

  1. Walk me through a DCF.
  2. How do you calculate WACC?
  3. Explain the difference between equity value and enterprise value.
  4. How would you value a private company?
  5. What are common multiples used in valuation?
  6. How do you handle negative cash flows in a DCF?
  7. Explain purchase price allocation (PPA).
  8. How do you value intangible assets?
  9. What’s different about valuing a startup?
  10. How do you approach portfolio valuation?

Each of these questions tests your ability to structure answers clearly and apply valuation concepts in real-world scenarios.
